The Story Behind the Stall

Why Bipul Da's "5 minutes" gets NIT Silchar students in trouble.

📍

The Place

Just 80 meters outside Gate 1 of NIT Silchar, Cachar, Assam. Bipul Da runs a humble roadside tin-shed canteen. During late night study hours and end-semester exams, dozens of students from Hostel 1 to 9 walk over in the cold for hot Maggi and Chai.

⏰

The 12:30 AM Curfew

The campus security guards shut Gate 1 at 12:30 AM sharp. Any student who arrives late is stopped, interrogated, and their roll number is written into the late-entry register. Repeat offenses mean notices to parents and hostel wardens.

🗣️

The "5 Minute" Habit

Whenever a student asks how long food will take, Bipul Da smiles and says: "Bas 5 minute lagega, bohi thaak!" He admits: if he tells students it takes 20 minutes, they will leave or walk to rival tea stalls.

🔥 Why Bipul Da Physically Cannot Cook Faster

Bipul Da cooks completely alone using only two gas burners:

  • Burner 1: Frying tawa for Bun Omelettes, buttered pao, and egg toasts.
  • Burner 2: Pot for boiling water and making Maggi or simmering tea.

When 6 or 8 students show up at 11:45 PM, he cannot magically cook 8 orders at once. Furthermore, whenever an order switches from Maggi to an omelette, he has to wipe down the tawa with a cloth, pour fresh oil, and whisk eggs in a steel cup. That creates 1 to 2 minutes of cleaning delay between orders that waiting students never see!

Key Insight: NightQueue doesn't ask Bipul Da to change his cooking. Instead, it gives students the honest truth so they never get locked out of their hostels.

How It Works & Simple Test

Understand the simple math and test how the app handles mistakes.

1. The Simple Formula

The calculation is very simple and realistic:

Total Time = (Walk to Gate 1) + (Real Cooking Wait) + (Walk back to Hostel)
Return Time = Current Time + Total Time
If Return Time is after 12:30 AM → You Miss Curfew!

Walking times are measured specifically for NIT Silchar: Hostel 1 is 5 minutes away, Hostel 7 is 8 minutes away, and PG Hostel 9 is 11 minutes away.

2. Error Testing (Handling Bad Inputs)

What happens if someone enters impossible numbers (like -5 people in queue, or 0 active burners)? The server automatically fixes bad values safely so the app never crashes.
