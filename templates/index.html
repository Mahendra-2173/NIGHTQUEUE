Why does Bipul Da always say "5 minutes"?

Bipul Da operates only 2 domestic gas burners. One holds a tawa for frying eggs and buns, and the other holds a kettle or pot for Maggi. When 4 to 6 people are already waiting, food takes 15 to 25 minutes. He quotes "5 minutes" so students don't walk away to competing stalls.

The Story of Bipul Da's Tea Stall ("Tapri")

A real roadside food stall 80 meters outside Gate 1, NIT Silchar, Cachar, Assam.

📷 Real Photos of the Stall & Setup

Photo 1: The Stall Front

The tin-shed stall located 80 meters outside NIT Silchar Gate 1. While the signboard reads "NIKHIL TEA STALL" (নিখিল টি স্টল) with Campa Energy boards, all students know it as Bipul Da's Tea Stall or simply "Tapri".

View through the wire mesh of the 2-burner domestic stove. Burner 1 keeps the tea kettle simmering or boils water for Maggi. Burner 2 holds a tawa for frying eggs and buns. With only 2 burners, cooking multiple dishes takes real time!

The person behind the stall
📍

The Place

Outside Gate 1 (Main Gate) of NIT Silchar. Hostellers from BH1 through BH9 and GH1 through GH4 walk down here after 11:00 PM when campus canteens shut down.

👨‍🍳

The Person

Bipul Nath ("Bipul Da"), who cooks and runs the stall. He works in the late-night cold, managing boiling pots, frying eggs, and toasting buns for hungry students.

⏰

The Real Problem

Boys hostels have a 12:30 AM curfew; girls hostels have a 10:30 PM curfew. Bipul Da always tells everyone "Bas 5 minute lagega!" so they don't leave. But with 4 to 8 people ahead, it actually takes 20 minutes, causing students to run or get locked out!

🗣️ Conversation with Bipul Da During Fieldwork

3 things Bipul Da told us:

  • "Bohot rush thake 11:30 r por. Shobai ekloge ashe, ami eka ranna kori." — (There is a huge rush after 11:30 PM. Everyone arrives at once, and I cook alone.)
  • "Bas 5 minute boila dei, noile student ra chole jabe dusra dokane." — (I tell them '5 minutes', otherwise students will get impatient and go to another stall.)
  • "Guard whistle bajay 12:20 te. Tokhon student ra chot-fot kore, order cancel kore." — (Hostel guards blow whistles at 12:20 AM. Then students panic and cancel orders after eggs are already cracked.)
One thing that surprised us:

Bipul Da isn't being dishonest on purpose. When he says "5 minutes", his mind is only counting the 4 minutes of actual cooking time on the stove! He completely overlooks the 15 minutes of orders waiting in line ahead of the new customer.
