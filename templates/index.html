Why does Bipul Da always say "5 minutes"?

Bipul Da operates only 2 domestic gas burners. One holds a tawa for frying eggs and buns, and the other holds a kettle or pot for Maggi. When 4 to 6 people are already waiting, food takes 15 to 25 minutes. He quotes "5 minutes" so students don't walk away to competing stalls.

The Story of Bipul Da's Tea Stall ("Tapri")

A real roadside food stall 80 meters outside Gate 1, NIT Silchar, Cachar, Assam.

📍

The Place

Outside Gate 1 (Main Gate) of NIT Silchar along the Silchar-Hailakandi Road. Hostellers from BH1 through BH9 and GH1 through GH4 walk down here after 11:00 PM when campus canteens shut down.

👨‍🍳

The People

Bipul Nath ("Bipul Da"), who manages the stove, and his helper Raju, who takes orders, serves hot plates, and manages the counter queue.

⏰

The Real Problem

Boys hostels have a 12:30 AM curfew; girls hostels have a 10:30 PM curfew. Bipul Da always tells everyone "Bas 5 minute!" so they don't leave. But with 4 to 8 people ahead, it takes 20 minutes, causing students to run or get locked out!

🗣️ Conversation with Bipul Da During Fieldwork

3 things Bipul Da told us (translated to Hindi):

  • "11:30 के बाद बहुत भारी भीड़ हो जाती है। सारे हॉस्टल के लड़के एक साथ आते हैं और चूल्हा सिर्फ दो ही है।"
    ("There is a huge rush after 11:30 PM. Everyone arrives at once, and I have only 2 burners.")
  • "अगर सबको बोल दूँ कि 20 मिनट लगेगा, तो छात्र सोचेंगे दुकान बंद हो रही है और चले जाएंगे। इसलिए मुँह से 5 मिनट निकल जाता है।"
    ("If I tell them 20 minutes, students think the shop is closing and leave. So I instinctively say '5 minutes'.")
  • "12:20 AM के बाद हॉस्टल गार्ड गेट 1 पर सीटी बजाने लगता है। तब सब लड़के घबरा जाते हैं और चूल्हे पर आकर सिर पर खड़े हो जाते हैं कि जल्दी दो।"
    ("After 12:20 AM, the guard blows whistles at Gate 1. Then students panic and crowd the stove demanding instant food.")
One thing that surprised us:

Bipul Da isn't being dishonest on purpose. When he says "5 minutes", his mind is only counting the 4 minutes of actual cooking time on the stove! He completely overlooks the 15 minutes of orders waiting in line ahead of the new customer.
