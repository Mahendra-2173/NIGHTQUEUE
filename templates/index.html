The Fieldwork Story

Actual problem context, location, vendor interviewed, and qualitative fieldwork findings.

📍

Actual Location

Roadside tin-shed stall, located exactly 80 meters outside Gate 1 (Main Gate) of National Institute of Technology (NIT) Silchar, Cachar, Assam (788010). Late-night hostellers walk across campus to this spot for dinner snacks.

👨‍🍳

Real Person Helped

Bipul Nath ("Bipul Da"), age 44, sole proprietor and cook. He runs the stall entirely alone, managing two commercial gas burners while cracking eggs and stirring Maggi pans in freezing late-night temperatures.

⏰

Actual Problem Statement

Hostellers face a strict 12:30 AM gate curfew. Because Bipul Da fears losing customers to rival stalls, he tells every student: "Bas 5 minute lagega, bohi thaak!" When 6+ orders queue up, cooking takes 20–28 minutes, resulting in panicked sprints and register penalties.

🔬 What Was Learned from the Fieldwork Interaction

During our on-site observation on November 14–15 (23:20 to 00:15 IST), we uncovered key operational insights that desk analysis would miss:

  • The 2-Burner Hardware Constraint: Bipul Da operates only two burners: Burner 1 holds a tawa for frying eggs and buns; Burner 2 holds a pot for boiling water/Maggi. Concurrency is capped at 2 orders at a time.
  • Pan Transition Dead Time: Switching between dish types (e.g., Egg Toast after Maggi) requires wiping down the tawa with a coarse rag, re-oiling, and egg whisking. This creates 60 to 90 seconds of zero-cooking overhead invisible to waiting students.
  • Why Existing Alternatives Fail: Food delivery apps (Swiggy/Zomato) do not service unregistered roadside stalls for ₹40 midnight snacks. Phone calls or WhatsApp cannot be answered while flipping hot eggs with one hand and boiling Maggi with the other.
  • The 12:20 AM Warning Whistle: Security guards blow whistles 10 minutes before the 12:30 AM gate closure. That is when anxious students begin shouting at the counter, demanding instant food or canceling orders after eggs are already cracked.
